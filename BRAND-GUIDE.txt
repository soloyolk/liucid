LIUCID — BRAND SYSTEM
Version 1.0 · 2026

BRAND IDEA
LIUCID is the holding company behind modern consumer ventures at the intersection of
technology, food, culture and everyday life.

CORE MARK
The LIU monogram combines a geometric L/E-like frame with 刘 (Liu), making the founder
heritage part of the symbol rather than a separate decorative element.

TYPOGRAPHY
Primary wordmark: use the supplied LIUCID wordmark artwork.
For supporting text, use a clean modern sans-serif such as Inter, Helvetica Neue,
Arial, or a comparable grotesk. Use generous tracking for uppercase labels.

LOCKUPS
1. Primary: LIU monogram + LIUCID wordmark + TECHNOLOGY × FOOD × LIFE.
2. Horizontal: monogram at left, LIUCID wordmark at right.
3. Icon-only: monogram for favicon, social avatar, app icon and small-format use.
4. Wordmark-only: use when the audience already knows the LIUCID identity.

USAGE
• Maintain clear space around the mark equal to at least 1/4 of the monogram height.
• Prefer gold-on-obsidian for premium/corporate applications.
• Use gold-on-warm-ivory for editorial, print and light-background applications.
• Use white-on-obsidian where gold reproduction is unavailable.
• Keep the 刘 character integrated with the monogram; do not separate or redraw it.
• Do not add shadows, gradients, outlines, or extra technology/circuit imagery.
